Camryn's Fairy Garden Lab

Camryn has a business idea: custom 3D printed garden disks that sit on top of soil in a plant pot. They can help block weeds, slow water from drying out on hot days, and make the top of the pot look magical instead of plain dirt.

🌸
🏈
🧚
1

Pick a theme

Fairy garden, flowers, sports team colors, tropical island, spooky-cute, animals, or a totally custom idea.

2

Choose plant holes

The disk could have one big hole or several smaller holes depending on the plant pot and design.

3

Print and decorate

Use a 3D printer, paint, multicolor filament, or other parent-approved coloring methods to finish the look.

4

Keep it parent-approved

Photos, prices, orders, customer messages, and selling should always be handled with adult help.
